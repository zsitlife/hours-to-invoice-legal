Privacy Policy

App: Hours to Invoice (com.hourstoinvoice.app)
Developer: zsitlife  |  Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hours to Invoice mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is a time-tracking and invoicing tool for freelancers and small businesses. It is designed to work without an account, and your business records stay on your device.

1. Information you create in the App

The App stores the data you enter — clients, projects, time entries, invoices, rates, currencies, company details and preferences — in a private database on your device. This data is not uploaded to our servers and is not synced to any account. It remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.

2. Information collected automatically

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how features are used and to improve reliability. Firebase Analytics may collect:

  • App usage events (for example, screens opened and features used);
  • App instance identifiers and device/app technical information (such as device model, operating system version, app version);
  • Approximate, non-precise location derived from IP address, used only in aggregate analytics.

We do not use this data to identify you personally, and we do not combine it with the business records you create in the App. Analytics data is processed by Google as a service provider. See Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Optional Pro features are offered as auto-renewing subscriptions. All payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We receive only the purchase status and a purchase token needed to unlock the subscription and to verify it with Google Play.

4. Advertising

The App does not contain third-party advertising and does not include an advertising SDK. We do not sell or rent personal information.

5. Data sharing

We do not share your business records with third parties. The only third-party processing involved is the Google services described above (Firebase Analytics and Google Play Billing), which act as service providers on our behalf.

6. Data security and retention

Your business records are kept in the App's private storage area on your device, protected by the Android operating system's app sandbox. You can delete individual records inside the App at any time. Uninstalling the App removes its local data. Analytics information is retained by Firebase according to Google's retention settings for the project.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for adults and business users and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices

You may stop the transmission of analytics data at any time by uninstalling the App or by disabling data usage for the App in your device settings. You may cancel a subscription through Google Play at any time.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at zsitlife0016@gmail.com.
