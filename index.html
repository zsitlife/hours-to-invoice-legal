Privacy Policy

App: Hours to Invoice (com.hourstoinvoice.app) Developer: zsitlife  |  Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hours to Invoice mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is a time-tracking and invoicing tool for freelancers and small businesses. It is designed to work without an account, and your business records stay on your device.

1. Information you create in the App

The App stores the data you enter — clients, projects, time entries, invoices, rates, currencies, company details and preferences — in a private database on your device. This data is not uploaded to our servers and is not synced to any account. It remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. You may also export it yourself as CSV or a full JSON backup.

2. Information collected automatically

The App uses Google Firebase services (Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config) to understand how features are used, to improve reliability, and to configure app behaviour. These services may collect:

  • App usage events (for example, screens opened and features used);
  • App instance identifiers and device/app technical information (such as device model, operating system version, app version);
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information when the App stops unexpectedly;
  • A device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID), which is also read by the Firebase SDK included in the App. This version of the App does not display ads; the identifier is retained so that optional advertising can be supported in a future version;
  • Approximate, non-precise location derived from IP address, used only in aggregate analytics.

We do not use this data to identify you personally, and we do not combine it with the business records you create in the App. Analytics and crash data are processed by Google as a service provider. See Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Optional Pro features are offered as auto-renewing subscriptions. All payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We receive only the purchase status and a purchase token needed to unlock the subscription and to verify it with Google Play.

4. Advertising

This version of the App does not display advertising. However, the App includes Google Firebase SDKs that may access the device advertising identifier, and we may introduce ads in a future version. We do not currently use the advertising identifier to build advertising profiles, and we never sell or rent personal information. You can reset or delete the advertising identifier at any time in your device settings.

5. Data sharing

We do not share your business records with third parties. The only third-party processing involved is the Google services described above (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config and Google Play Billing), which act as service providers on our behalf.

6. Data security and retention

Your business records are kept in the App's private storage area on your device, protected by the Android operating system's app sandbox. You can delete individual records inside the App at any time. Uninstalling the App removes its local data. Analytics and crash information is retained by Firebase according to Google's retention settings for the project.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for adults and business users and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices

You may stop the transmission of analytics data at any time by uninstalling the App or by disabling data usage for the App in your device settings. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android settings. You may cancel a subscription through Google Play at any time.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at zsitlife0016@gmail.com.
